Privacy policy

PHB70 takes the protection of your personal data very seriously. This policy explains what data is processed when you visit this website or write to us, why, and how to exercise your rights, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act (loi Informatique et Libertés).

This English translation is provided for information only. In the event of any discrepancy, the French version prevails.

Data controller

The data controller is PHB 70, a single-shareholder simplified joint-stock company with a share capital of €20,000, whose registered office is at 6 boulevard des Grands Horizons, Villa Jaye, 83120 Sainte-Maxime, France, registered with the Fréjus Trade and Companies Register under number 910 749 936.

Contact for any question about your data: [TO BE COMPLETED: dedicated e-mail address for personal data requests].

Data collected

Through the contact form: full name, e-mail address and message (required); company, phone number and project type (optional). Please do not include any sensitive data in your message.

While browsing: as with any website, your IP address and technical information (browser type, pages requested, date and time) are sent to the server hosting the website and to the services that provide certain page resources (see “Recipients and processors”).

The website uses no audience measurement tool, no advertising tracker and no embedded social network.

Purposes and legal bases

Purpose Legal basis
Responding to your contact, information or quote requests, and following up on your project Pre-contractual measures taken at your request (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for other requests, our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us (art. 6(1)(f))
Displaying the website and ensuring its security and proper technical operation Our legitimate interest in providing an available and secure website (art. 6(1)(f))

Your data is never sold, nor used for marketing purposes without your consent.

Recipients and processors

Your data is intended only for the people at PHB 70 in charge of handling your request. It may be shared with the following service providers, strictly within the scope of their mission:

  • Website hosting: Cloudflare, Inc. (United States).
  • Delivery of form messages: Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc., United States), an e-mail delivery service that forwards your request to PHB 70.
  • Mailbox receiving requests: [TO BE COMPLETED: PHB 70 e-mail provider].

Transfers outside the European Union

Our host, Cloudflare, Inc., is based in the United States. It may process certain data, including your IP address, outside the European Union.

These transfers are covered by Cloudflare’s certification under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, recognised by the European Commission’s adequacy decision of 10 July 2023, and by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses included in its data processing agreement.

Messages sent through the contact form are delivered by Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.), a company based in the United States. They are processed from its European region (Ireland), but access from the United States cannot be ruled out. Any such transfers are covered by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses included in its data processing agreement.

Retention periods

  • Contact requests not followed up: 3 years from the last exchange, then deleted.
  • Requests that lead to a project: for the duration of the business relationship, then 5 years from its end, corresponding to the applicable limitation period (article L110-4 of the French Commercial Code). Invoices and accounting records are kept for 10 years, in accordance with article L123-22 of the French Commercial Code.
  • Copies of messages held by our delivery provider: Resend keeps a technical copy of delivered e-mails for a limited period set by its retention policy, after which it is automatically deleted.
  • Server technical logs: kept for a limited period by our host Cloudflare, in accordance with its privacy policy; PHB 70 keeps no copy.

Cookies and trackers

The website sets no advertising or audience measurement cookies. Only one technical item is stored in your browser (“session storage”) so that the opening logo animation is not replayed every time you return to the home page. It contains no personal data, is shared with no one and is automatically deleted when you close the tab. As it is strictly necessary for the interface to work, it does not require your consent (article 82 of the French Data Protection Act).

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict and object to its processing, and to data portability, as well as the right to set guidelines on what happens to your data after your death.

To exercise these rights, write to us at [TO BE COMPLETED: dedicated e-mail address for personal data requests] or by post to PHB 70, 6 boulevard des Grands Horizons, Villa Jaye, 83120 Sainte-Maxime, France. You will receive a reply within one month. Proof of identity may be requested in case of reasonable doubt.

If, after contacting us, you believe your rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL), 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France — www.cnil.fr.

Security

PHB70 implements appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, unauthorised access or disclosure. The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and form messages are sent to our delivery provider over an encrypted connection as well.
