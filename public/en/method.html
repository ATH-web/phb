Our method

From idea to finished piece

Four steps to turn your project into exceptional furniture, with a single point of contact from the first sketch to delivery.

Step 01

Feasibility & technical design

A piece comes to us as a sketch, an inspiration photo or an architect’s drawing. Our design office checks that it can be made, then translates it into shop drawings: dimensions, sections, joints, materials and finishes, all designed to last in daily use.

You know the price and lead time very early on, and we choose, from our network of partner workshops, the one that best masters the required craft.

Step 02

Prototype

Before production starts, we make a prototype. Proportions, comfort, how the materials hold up, how the finishes look: everything is checked for real, and adjusted if needed.

You approve the piece exactly as it will be delivered.

Step 03

Production

Once the prototype is approved, the series is launched. Our local teams, close to the manufacturing sites, validate the processes and monitor progress every day.

Every piece in the series is identical to the one you approved.

Step 04

Inspection & delivery

Every piece is inspected before shipping, then protected in suitable packaging. Our administrative team handles logistics and customs formalities.

Your furniture arrives ready to install, wherever your project is.
