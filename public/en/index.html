Our mission

Bringing your project to life

PHB70 designs and manufactures bespoke furniture for hotels, restaurants and high-end residences.

Our network of craftsmen and partner workshops, built over more than 20 years, allows us to deliver the boldest projects. We favour short supply chains to keep costs and lead times under control. Our in-house design office follows every piece, from prototype to quality control before shipping.
