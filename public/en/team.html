The team

Our team

Designers, craftsmen and project managers: one team sharing the same high standards, from the first sketch to the installation of the very last piece.

The founder

Philippe Boccara

PHB70 was founded by Philippe Boccara, a French entrepreneur based in New York with recognised expertise in manufacturing and distribution networks. His approach, shaped across several international companies, favours short supply chains to optimise costs and lead times — for the benefit of hospitality and restaurant professionals.

Project manager

Goreti

Goreti is the link between the client and the workshop. She turns each request into precise production instructions, follows every stage through to delivery and keeps you updated without waiting to be asked — in French, Portuguese or English, depending on who is across the table. A client who calls always speaks to someone who knows their file.

Project manager

Bruno

Bruno runs operations and production. He selects the workshops piece by piece, then stays on the production floor to settle a section, a shade or a joint — and have it corrected before the series is made. He keeps schedules on track, constantly looks for the technical solution that saves time without compromising execution, and personally inspects every batch through to delivery.

Interior architect & decorator

Laure

Laure helps private clients lay out and furnish their homes. She arranges rooms, selects furniture and composes atmospheres by combining materials, colours and finishes. She also designs bespoke furniture, adjusting shapes and proportions to the way each space is used and to its architecture.

From the first sketch to the finishing details, Laure bridges what she imagines with her clients and what the craftsmen create in the workshop.
